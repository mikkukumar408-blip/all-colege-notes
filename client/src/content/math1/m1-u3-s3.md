### 1. Total Differential, Chain Rule & Extrema of Multivariable Functions

#### A. The Total Differential ($du$) & Total Derivative ($\frac{du}{dt}$):
- **Total Differential:** $du = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} dx + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} dy + \frac{\partial u}{\partial z} dz$
- **Total Derivative (Single Parameter $t$):**
  $$\mathbf{\frac{du}{dt} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt}}$$
- **Composite Chain Rule ($x = x(r, \theta), \; y = y(r, \theta)$):**
  $$\mathbf{\frac{\partial u}{\partial r} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial x}{\partial r} + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\frac{\partial y}{\partial r}}, \quad \mathbf{\frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta}}$$

### 2. Unconstrained Maxima & Minima of Two Variables ($rt - s^2$ Test)

To locate the local maxima, local minima, and saddle points of $z = f(x, y)$:
1. **Stationary Points:** Solve $f_x = 0$ and $f_y = 0$.
2. **Second-Order Partials at $(a, b)$:** $r = f_{xx}, \; s = f_{xy}, \; t = f_{yy}$ and Discriminant $\mathbf{\Delta = rt - s^2}$:

| Condition on $\Delta = rt - s^2$ | Condition on $r = f_{xx}$ | Nature of Critical Point $(a, b)$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\mathbf{rt - s^2 > 0}$** | $\mathbf{r < 0}$ | **Local Maximum** |
| **$\mathbf{rt - s^2 > 0}$** | $\mathbf{r > 0}$ | **Local Minimum** |
| **$\mathbf{rt - s^2 < 0}$** | Any sign | **Saddle Point** |
| **$\mathbf{rt - s^2 = 0}$** | Indeterminate | **Test Fails** (Further investigation required) |

### 3. Constrained Optimization: Method of Lagrange Multipliers

To optimize $f(x, y, z)$ subject to constraint $\phi(x, y, z) = 0$:
1. Form Auxiliary Lagrangian: $\mathbf{F(x, y, z, \lambda) = f(x, y, z) + \lambda \, \phi(x, y, z)}$.
2. Solve the stationary system:
   $$\mathbf{f_x + \lambda \phi_x = 0, \quad f_y + \lambda \phi_y = 0, \quad f_z + \lambda \phi_z = 0, \quad \phi(x, y, z) = 0}$$

**University Question (4 Marks):**
Find the local maximum and minimum values of $f(x, y) = x^3 + y^3 - 3xy$.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Find Critical (Stationary) Points**
$f_x = 3x^2 - 3y = 0 \implies y = x^2$ and $f_y = 3y^2 - 3x = 0 \implies 3x(x^3 - 1) = 0$.
Critical points: $(0, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$.

**Step 2 ⚡: Compute Second Partials $r, s, t$**
$r = f_{xx} = 6x, \quad s = f_{xy} = -3, \quad t = f_{yy} = 6y \implies \Delta = rt - s^2 = 36xy - 9$.

**Step 3 🎯: Classify Points**
1. **At $(0, 0)$:** $\Delta = 0 - 9 = -9 < 0 \implies (0, 0)$ is a **Saddle Point**.
2. **At $(1, 1)$:** $\Delta = 36 - 9 = 27 > 0$ and $r = 6 > 0 \implies (1, 1)$ is a **Local Minimum** with $f_{\min}(1, 1) = 1 + 1 - 3 = -1$.

<div class="example-answer-box">
🎯 Saddle point: $(0, 0)$ | Local Minimum at $(1, 1)$ with $\mathbf{f_{\min} = -1}$.
</div>

**University Question (6 Marks - MMDU Question Paper):**
Find the maximum volume of a rectangular box open at the top, having a given surface area $S = 108\,\text{cm}^2$, using Lagrange's method of multipliers.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Objective and Constraint Functions**
Maximize $V = xyz$ subject to $\phi(x, y, z) = xy + 2yz + 2zx - 108 = 0$.
Lagrangian: $F(x, y, z, \lambda) = xyz + \lambda(xy + 2yz + 2zx - 108)$.

**Step 2 ⚡: Set First Partials to Zero**
$$yz + \lambda(y + 2z) = 0, \quad xz + \lambda(x + 2z) = 0, \quad xy + \lambda(2x + 2y) = 0$$
Equating $\lambda$ gives $x = y = 2z \implies z = \frac{x}{2}$.

**Step 3 🎯: Substitute into Constraint $S = 108$**
$$x^2 + 2x(x/2) + 2x(x/2) = 3x^2 = 108 \implies x = 6, \; y = 6, \; z = 3 \implies V_{\max} = 6 \times 6 \times 3 = 108\,\text{cm}^3$$

<div class="example-answer-box">
🎯 Dimensions: $6\,\text{cm} \times 6\,\text{cm} \times 3\,\text{cm}$ | Maximum Volume: $\mathbf{V_{\max} = 108\,\text{cm}^3}$.
</div>

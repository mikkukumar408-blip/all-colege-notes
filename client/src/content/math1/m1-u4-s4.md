### 1. Even/Odd Symmetries, Change of Interval $(-l, l)$ & Half-Range Series

| Parity / Domain | Fourier Coefficients | Series Expansion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Even Function in $(-l, l)$** | $b_n = 0$, $a_0 = \frac{2}{l}\int_0^l f(x)dx$, $a_n = \frac{2}{l}\int_0^l f(x)\cos\frac{n\pi x}{l}dx$ | $f(x) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n \cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{l}\right)$ |
| **Odd Function in $(-l, l)$** | $a_0 = 0, a_n = 0$, $b_n = \frac{2}{l}\int_0^l f(x)\sin\frac{n\pi x}{l}dx$ | $f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{l}\right)$ |
| **Half-Range Cosine in $(0, l)$** | $b_n = 0$, $a_0 = \frac{2}{l}\int_0^l f(x)dx$, $a_n = \frac{2}{l}\int_0^l f(x)\cos\frac{n\pi x}{l}dx$ | Even periodic extension of period $2l$ |
| **Half-Range Sine in $(0, l)$** | $a_0 = 0, a_n = 0$, $b_n = \frac{2}{l}\int_0^l f(x)\sin\frac{n\pi x}{l}dx$ | Odd periodic extension of period $2l$ |

---

**University Question (6 Marks - MMDU Question Paper):**
Find the Fourier series for the triangular wave $f(x) = l - |x|$ in the arbitrary interval $(-l, l)$. Hence deduce $\frac{1}{1^2} + \frac{1}{3^2} + \frac{1}{5^2} + \dots = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Symmetry Check in $(-l, l)$**
Since $f(-x) = l - |-x| = f(x)$, $f(x)$ is **even** $\implies b_n = 0$ and $a_0 = \frac{2}{l} \int_0^l (l - x) dx = l$.

**Step 2 ⚡: Compute $a_n$ and Deduce at $x = 0$**
$$a_n = \frac{2}{l} \int_0^l (l - x) \cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{l}\right) dx = \frac{2l}{n^2\pi^2}[1 - (-1)^n] = \begin{cases} \frac{4l}{n^2\pi^2}, & n \text{ odd} \\ 0, & n \text{ even} \end{cases}$$
At $x = 0$: $l = \frac{l}{2} + \frac{4l}{\pi^2}\left(\frac{1}{1^2} + \frac{1}{3^2} + \frac{1}{5^2} + \dots\right) \implies \sum_{n=1,3,5\dots}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$.

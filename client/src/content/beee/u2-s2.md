### 1. In-Depth Analysis of Transformer Losses & Efficiency

#### A. Core Losses / Iron Losses ($P_i$ — Invariant with Load):
Occur inside the magnetic core due to cyclic reversal of magnetization:
1. **Hysteresis Loss ($P_h$):** Energy dissipated aligning molecular magnetic dipoles:
   $$\mathbf{P_h = \eta_h · B_m^{1.6} · f · V_{\text{core}}}    [\text{Steinmetz Equation}]$$
   - Minimized by using **Cold-Rolled Grain-Oriented (CRGO) Silicon Steel** with a narrow hysteresis loop.
2. **Eddy Current Loss ($P_e$):** Joule heating from induced circular eddy currents in the conductive core:
   $$\mathbf{P_e = K_e · B_m^2 · f^2 · t² · V_{\text{core}}}$$
   - Minimized by slicing the solid core into **thin insulated laminations** of thickness $t ≈ 0.25 - 0.35~\text{mm}$ varnished on both sides.

#### B. Copper Losses ($P_{cu}$ — Variable with Load):
Joule heating in the electrical windings:
$$\mathbf{P_{cu} = I₁^2 R₁ + I₂^2 R₂ = I₂^2 R_{eq2} = x² P_{cu~\text{FL}}}$$
where $x = \frac{I₂}{I_{2~\text{FL}}} = \frac{\text{Load kVA}}{\text{Rated kVA}}$ is the fractional loading factor.

---

### 2. Efficiency Formulation & Maximum Efficiency Derivation

$$\mathbf{\eta = \frac{\text{Output Power}}{\text{Input Power}} = \frac{x · S_{\text{rated}} · \cos\phi_2}{x · S_{\text{rated}} · \cos\phi_2 + P_i + x² P_{cu~\text{FL}}}}$$

#### Condition for Maximum Efficiency:
Differentiating $\eta$ with respect to load current $I₂$ and setting to zero:
$$\frac{d\eta}{dI_2} = 0 ⇒ \mathbf{P_i = x² P_{cu~\text{FL}} \iff \text{Constant Iron Loss} = \text{Variable Copper Loss}}$$
- Fractional load at maximum efficiency:
  $$\mathbf{x_{\max} = \sqrt{\frac{P_i}{P_{cu~\text{FL}}}}}$$
- Load kVA for maximum efficiency:
  $$\mathbf{\text{kVA}_{\max} = \text{Rated kVA} \times \sqrt{\frac{P_i}{P_{cu~\text{FL}}}}}$$

---

### 3. Open Circuit (OC) and Short Circuit (SC) Test Formulations

| Test Metric | Open Circuit (OC) Test | Short Circuit (SC) Test |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Objective** | Determine Core Loss $P_i$, $R_0$, and $X_0$ | Determine Full-Load Copper Loss $P_{cu}$, $R_{eq}$, $X_{eq}$ |
| **Winding Energized** | Low Voltage (LV) winding | High Voltage (HV) winding |
| **Opposite Winding** | High Voltage left open ($I₂ = 0$) | Low Voltage shorted with thick copper conductor |
| **Excitation Level** | Rated rated voltage $V₁$ at rated frequency $f$ | Small reduced voltage ($5% - 8%$ of rated) |
| **Key Formulae** | $\cos\phi_0 = \frac{W_{oc}}{V₁ I_0}$$I_c = I_0 \cos\phi_0 ⇒ R_0 = \frac{V₁}{I_c}$$I_m = I_0 \sin\phi_0 ⇒ X_0 = \frac{V₁}{I_m}$ | $R_{eq} = \frac{W_{sc}}{I_{sc}^2}$$Z_{eq} = \frac{V_{sc}}{I_{sc}}$$X_{eq} = \sqrt{Z_{eq}^2 - R_{eq}^2}$ |

---

**Problem:**
A $10~\text{kVA}$ transformer has iron loss $P_i = 150~\text{W}$ and full-load copper loss $P_{cu} = 250~\text{W}$. Calculate its full-load efficiency at unity power factor ($\cos\phi = 1.0$).

---

**Step 1 🚀: Output Power**
$$P_{\text{out}} = \text{kVA} \times 1000 \times \cos\phi = 10 \times 1000 \times 1.0 = \mathbf{10{,}000~\text{Watts}}$$

**Step 2 ⚡: Total Losses**
$$P_{\text{loss}} = P_i + P_{cu} = 150 + 250 = \mathbf{400~\text{Watts}}$$

**Step 3 🎯: Efficiency Formula**
$$\eta = \frac{P_{\text{out}}}{P_{\text{out}} + P_{\text{loss}}} \times 100 = \frac{10000}{10000 + 400} \times 100 = \frac{10000}{10400} \times 100 = \mathbf{96.15\%}$$


🎯 Full-Load Efficiency: $\mathbf{\eta = 96.15\%}$.




**University Question (8 Marks):**
A 25,kVA transformer has iron loss Pᵢ = 200,W and full-load copper loss Pcᵤ = 400,W. Find:
1. Efficiency at half-load with 0.8 power factor lagging.
2. The load kVA at which maximum efficiency occurs.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Half-Load (x = 0.5) Calculation**
- Output power: Pₒᵤₜ = (0.5)(25 × 10³)(0.8) = 10{,000,W}
- Copper loss at half-load: Pcᵤ' = x² Pcᵤ = (0.5)² × 400 = 0.25 × 400 = 100,W
- Constant iron loss: Pᵢ = 200,W
- Efficiency:
  η₅₀% = \frac{10000}{10000 + 100 + 200} × 100 = \frac{10000}{10300} × 100 = 97.09%

**Step 2 ⚡: Load kVA for Maximum Efficiency (Pcᵤ = Pᵢ)**
kVAₘₐₓ = Rated kVA × √((Pᵢ / Pcᵤ)) = 25 × √(\frac{200}{400}) = 25 × √(0.5) = 25 × 0.7071 = 17.68,kVA


🎯 Half-Load Efficiency = $97.09%$ | Maximum Efficiency occurs at $\mathbf{17.68~\text{kVA}}$.

**Step 1 🚀: OC Test Parameters (Shunt Branch $R_0, X_0$)**
$$\cos\phi_0 = \frac{W_{oc}}{V_{oc} I_{oc}} = \frac{60}{200 \times 0.7} = \frac{60}{140} = 0.4286$$
$$\sin\phi_0 = \sqrt{1 - (0.4286)^2} = 0.9035$$
- Core loss current: $I_c = I_{oc} \cos\phi_0 = 0.7 \times 0.4286 = 0.30~\text{A}$
- Magnetizing current: $I_m = I_{oc} \sin\phi_0 = 0.7 \times 0.9035 = 0.632~\text{A}$
$$R_0 = \frac{V_{oc}}{I_c} = \frac{200}{0.30} = \mathbf{666.7~\Omega}$$
$$X_0 = \frac{V_{oc}}{I_m} = \frac{200}{0.632} = \mathbf{316.5~\Omega}$$

**Step 2 ⚡: SC Test Parameters (Series Branch referred to HV side)**
$$R_{eq2} = \frac{W_{sc}}{I_{sc}^2} = \frac{120}{(12.5)^2} = \frac{120}{156.25} = \mathbf{0.768~\Omega}$$
$$Z_{eq2} = \frac{V_{sc}}{I_{sc}} = \frac{22}{12.5} = \mathbf{1.76~\Omega}$$
$$X_{eq2} = \sqrt{Z_{eq2}^2 - R_{eq2}^2} = \sqrt{(1.76)^2 - (0.768)^2} = \sqrt{3.098 - 0.590} = \mathbf{1.584~\Omega}$$

**Step 3 🎯: Refer Series Branch to LV Side ($K = 400/200 = 2$)**
$$R_{eq1} = \frac{R_{eq2}}{K^2} = \frac{0.768}{4} = \mathbf{0.192~\Omega}$$
$X_{eq1} = \frac{X_{eq2}}{K^2} = \frac{1.584}{4} = \mathbf{0.396~\Omega}$

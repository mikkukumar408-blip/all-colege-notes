### 1. Complex Impedance & Series R-L-C Network Analysis

In the frequency domain (phasor representation), circuit elements are characterized by complex impedances:
- **Resistor ($R$):** $Z_R = R \angle 0^\circ = R + j0$. Voltage and current are strictly in phase ($\phi = 0^\circ$).
- **Inductor ($L$):** $Z_L = j\omega L = \omega L \angle +90^\circ$. Inductive reactance $X_L = 2π f L,[\Omega]$. Voltage leads current by $+90^\circ$.
- **Capacitor ($C$):** $Z_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C} = -j\frac{1}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{\omega C}\angle -90^\circ$. Capacitive reactance $X_C = \frac{1}{2π f C},[\Omega]$. Voltage lags current by $-90^\circ$.

#### Total Series Impedance:
$$\mathbf{Z = R + j(X_L - X_C) = |Z| \angle \phi}$$
$$\mathbf{|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2}},      \mathbf{\phi = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{X_L - X_C}{R}\right)}$$

---

### 2. Theoretical Analysis of Series Electrical Resonance

> 🟢 **Resonance Definition:** Series resonance occurs in an R-L-C circuit at the particular frequency where the net reactive component of the total impedance vanishes identically:
> $$\mathbf{\text{Im}(Z) = X_L - X_C = 0 \iff X_L = X_C}$$

#### Derivation of Resonant Frequency ($f_0$):
$$2π f_0 L = \frac{1}{2π f_0 C} ⇒ f_0^2 = \frac{1}{4π^2 L C} ⇒ \mathbf{f_0 = \frac{1}{2π\sqrt{LC}}},    \mathbf{\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}}$$

#### Phenomenological Characteristics at Resonance:
1. **Minimum Impedance:** $Z_{\min} = R$ (purely resistive, real).
2. **Maximum Current (Acceptor Circuit):** $I_0 = \frac{V}{Z_{\min}} = \frac{V}{R}$.
3. **Unity Power Factor:** $\cos\phi = \frac{R}{|Z|} = \frac{R}{R} = 1.0$.
4. **Voltage Magnification:** Although net reactive voltage is zero ($V_L + V_C = 0$), the individual voltages across inductor and capacitor can reach destructive levels:
   $$V_L = I_0 X_L = \left(\frac{V}{R}\right) (\omega_0 L) = Q · V,      V_C = I_0 X_C = Q · V$$

---

### 3. Quality Factor ($Q$), Bandwidth & Selectivity

The **Quality Factor ($Q$)** measures the sharp selectivity of the circuit:
$$\mathbf{Q = \frac{\text{Reactive Power}}{\text{Active Power}} = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega_0 C R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}}$$
- **Half-Power Frequencies ($f_1, f_2$):** Frequencies where current drops to $I_0 / \sqrt{2}$ ($70.7%$) and power drops by $50%$.
- **Bandwidth ($BW$):**
  $$\mathbf{BW = \Delta f = f_2 - f_1 = \frac{f_0}{Q} = \frac{R}{2π L}}$$

**Problem:**
A coil has resistance $R = 30~\Omega$ and inductive reactance $X_L = 40~\Omega$ across a $200~\text{V}, 50~\text{Hz}$ supply. Find total impedance $Z$, current $I$, and power factor $\cos\phi$.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Impedance Magnitude**
$$|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} = \sqrt{30^2 + 40^2} = \sqrt{900 + 1600} = \sqrt{2500} = \mathbf{50.0~\Omega}$$

**Step 2 ⚡: Current Magnitude**
$$I = \frac{V}{|Z|} = \frac{200~\text{V}}{50~\Omega} = \mathbf{4.0~\text{Amperes}}$$

**Step 3 🎯: Power Factor**
$$\cos\phi = \frac{R}{|Z|} = \frac{30}{50} = \mathbf{0.60~\text{lagging}}$$


🎯 Impedance $Z = 50.0~\Omega$ | Current $I = 4.0~\text{A}$ | Power Factor = $0.60$ (lagging).




**University Question (8 Marks):**
A coil of R = 10~\Omega, L = 0.15,H, and C = 100,μF is connected across 230,V, 50,Hz.
Find: (1) Total impedance at 50,Hz, (2) Resonant frequency f₀, (3) Peak current at resonance I₀.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Reactances at $50~\text{Hz}$**
$$X_L = 2\pi (50)(0.15) = \mathbf{47.12,Omega}, \quad X_C = \frac{1}{2\pi (50)(100 \times 10^{-6})} = \mathbf{31.83,Omega}$$
$$|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2} = \sqrt{10^2 + (47.12 - 31.83)^2} = \sqrt{100 + 233.78} = \mathbf{18.27,Omega}$$

**Step 2 ⚡: Resonant Frequency $f_0$**
$$f_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{0.15 \times 100 \times 10^{-6}}} = \frac{1}{2\pi (0.00387)} = \mathbf{41.09~\text{Hz}}$$

**Step 3 🎯: Current at Resonance**
$$\text{At resonance: } X_L = X_C \implies Z_{min} = R = 10,Omega$$
$$I_0 = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{230}{10} = \mathbf{23.0~\text{Amperes}}$$


🎯 At 50Hz: $Z = 18.27~\Omega$ | Resonant Frequency $f_0 = 41.09~\text{Hz}$ | Resonant Current $I_0 = 23.0~\text{A}$.




**University Question (10 Marks - University Exam):**
A series resonant circuit with R = 5~\Omega, L = 0.2,H, and C = 5,μF is connected to a 50,V variable frequency supply.
Determine: (1) Resonant frequency f₀, (2) Quality factor Q, (3) Bandwidth BW, (4) Voltage across the capacitor VC at resonance.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Resonant Frequency**
f₀ = \frac{1}{2}π√(0.2 × 5 × 10⁻⁶)) = \frac{1}{2}π√(10⁻⁶)) = \frac{1000}{2π} = 159.15,Hz
ω₀ = 2π f₀ = 1000,ad/s

**Step 2 ⚡: Quality Factor (Q)**
Q = (ω₀ L / R) = ((1000)(0.2) / 5) = \frac{200}{5} = 40.0

**Step 3 🎯: Bandwidth (BW)**
BW = (f₀ / Q) = (159.15 / 40) = 3.98,Hz

**Step 4 🏁: Voltage Magnification Across Capacitor at Resonance**
At resonance, circuit current is I₀ = (V / R) = \frac{50}{5} = 10.0,A.
Capacitive reactance at resonance:
XC = (1 / ω₀ C) = (1 / (1000)(5 × 10⁻⁶)) = 200~\Omega
VC = I₀ × XC = 10.0 × 200 = 2000.0,Volts = Q × Vₛᵤₚₚₗy = 40 × 50 = 2000,V
*(The voltage across the capacitor is 40 times higher than the input voltage!)*


🎯 $f_0 = 159.15~\text{Hz}$ | Quality Factor $Q = 40.0$ | Bandwidth $BW = 3.98~\text{Hz}$ | Resonant Capacitor Voltage $V_C = 2000~\text{V}$ (40x Magnification).

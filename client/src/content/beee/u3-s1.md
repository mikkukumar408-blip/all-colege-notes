### 1. Energy Band Theory & Semiconductor Physics

Solids are classified by the width of the forbidden energy bandgap ($E_g$) separating the completely filled **Valence Band** from the empty **Conduction Band**:
- **Metals (Conductors):** Valence and conduction bands overlap ($E_g = 0~\text{eV}$). Massive sea of free electrons.
- **Insulators (Diamond, Quartz):** Very wide bandgap ($E_g > 5~\text{eV}$). Extremely low thermal excitation across gap.
- **Semiconductors (Silicon, Germanium):** Moderate bandgap:
  $$E_g(\text{Si}) ≈ 1.12~\text{eV},      E_g(\text{Ge}) ≈ 0.67~\text{eV}    (\text{at } 300~\text{K})$$

---

### 2. Intrinsic vs Extrinsic Semiconductors & Doping

1. **Intrinsic Semiconductor:** Chemically ultra-pure silicon ($99.9999999%$).
   - Thermal excitation generates electron-hole pairs: $n = p = n_i$.
   - At room temperature ($300~\text{K}$), $n_i ≈ 1.5 \times 10^{10}~\text{cm}^{-3}$ for Silicon.
2. **Extrinsic (Doped) Semiconductors:**
   - **N-Type Semiconductor:** Pure silicon doped with Pentavalent impurity atoms (Phosphorus, Arsenic, Antimony). 5 valence electrons $→$ 4 form covalent bonds, 1 is easily freed into conduction band. Majority carriers = **Electrons** ($n ≈ N_D$), Minority carriers = Holes.
   - **P-Type Semiconductor:** Pure silicon doped with Trivalent impurity atoms (Boron, Gallium, Indium). 3 valence electrons $→$ creates a vacant bond state (**Hole**). Majority carriers = **Holes** ($p ≈ N_A$), Minority carriers = Electrons.

---

### 3. The Law of Mass Action & Fermi-Dirac Statistics

> 🟢 **Mass Action Law:** In any non-degenerate semiconductor at thermal equilibrium, the product of electron and hole concentrations is strictly constant, depending only on temperature and bandgap, regardless of doping:
> $$\mathbf{n · p = n_i^2}$$

#### Fermi Energy Level ($E_F$):
The energy level at which the Fermi-Dirac probability of electron occupancy is exactly $50%$ ($f(E_F) = 0.5$):
- **Intrinsic:** $E_F ≈ E_i$ lies virtually at the exact center of the bandgap: $E_i = \frac{E_C + E_V}{2}$.
- **N-Type:** Doping with donors pushes $E_F$ upward toward the conduction band:
  $$\mathbf{E_F - E_i = k_B T \ln\left(\frac{N_D}{n_i}\right)}$$
- **P-Type:** Doping with acceptors pulls $E_F$ downward toward the valence band:
  $$\mathbf{E_i - E_F = k_B T \ln\left(\frac{N_A}{n_i}\right)}$$

**Problem:**
In a pure silicon semiconductor at $300~\text{K}$, the intrinsic carrier concentration is $n_i = 1.5 \times 10^{10}~\text{cm}^{-3}$. The sample is doped with donor atoms to a concentration of $N_D = 10^{16}~\text{cm}^{-3}$. Find the majority electron concentration $n$ and minority hole concentration $p$.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Majority Carrier Concentration**
Since $N_D \gg n_i$, virtually all donor atoms are ionized:
$$n ≈ N_D = \mathbf{1.0 \times 10^{16}~\text{cm}^{-3}}$$

**Step 2 ⚡: Apply Mass Action Law $n · p = n_i^2$**
$$p = \frac{n_i^2}{n} = \frac{(1.5 \times 10^{10})^2}{10^{16}} = \frac{2.25 \times 10^{20}}{10^{16}} = \mathbf{2.25 \times 10^4~\text{cm}^{-3}}$$


🎯 Electrons $n = 10^{16}~\text{cm}^{-3}$ | Holes $p = 2.25 \times 10^4~\text{cm}^{-3}$ (Drastic suppression of minority carriers).




**University Question (8 Marks):**
Pure silicon at room temperature has nᵢ = 1.5 × 10¹⁶,m⁻³, electron mobility μₙ = 0.135,m²/V·s, and hole mobility μₚ = 0.048,m²/V·s.
1. Find intrinsic conductivity σᵢ.
2. Find conductivity after doping with 10²² donor atoms/m³.

---

**Step 1 🚀: Intrinsic Conductivity σᵢ = nᵢ e (μₙ + μₚ)**
σᵢ = (1.5 × 10¹⁶)(1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)(0.135 + 0.048) = (2.4 × 10⁻³)(0.183) = 4.39 × 10⁻⁴,S/ᵐ

**Step 2 ⚡: Doped N-Type Conductivity σₙ ≈ ND e μₙ**
σₙ = (10²²)(1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)(0.135) = (1600)(0.135) = 216.0,S/m
Notice that doping by a tiny fraction increased electrical conductivity by **nearly 500,000 times**!


🎯 Intrinsic Conductivity $\sigma_i = 4.39 \times 10^{-4}~\text{S/m}$ | Doped Conductivity $\sigma_n = 216.0~\text{S/m}$.




**University Question (10 Marks):**
Calculate the displacement of the Fermi energy level EF from the intrinsic level Eᵢ in silicon at T = 300,K doped with 2.0 × 10¹⁶,cm⁻³ phosphorus atoms (nᵢ = 1.5 × 10¹⁰,cm⁻³, kB T = 0.0259,eV).

---

**Step 1 🚀: Fermi Level Offset Equation**
$$E_F - E_i = k_B T \ln\left(\frac{N_D}{n_i}\right)$$

**Step 2 ⚡: Compute Concentration Ratio**
(ND / nᵢ) = (2.0 × 10¹⁶ / 1.5 × 10¹⁰) = 1.333 × 10⁶

**Step 3 🎯: Evaluate Logarithm and Energy Shift**
\ln(1.333 × 10⁶) = \ln(1.333) + 6 \ln(10) = 0.2877 + 6(2.3026) = 0.2877 + 13.8155 = 14.103
EF - Eᵢ = (0.0259,eV) × 14.103 = 0.365,eV
The Fermi level shifts **0.365,eV upward toward the conduction band**.


🎯 Fermi Level Shift: $\mathbf{E_F - E_i = +0.365~\text{eV}}$ (closer to Conduction Band).
